01

The central story

Geralt, a professional monster hunter known as a Witcher, searches for Ciri—his former ward and a young woman with extraordinary powers. He is drawn into a dangerous pursuit while the mysterious spectral force known as the Wild Hunt closes in.

The main story combines a personal search with political conflict and an approaching supernatural threat. This summary avoids major spoilers.

02

A world shaped by war

The Northern Kingdoms are caught in a devastating war, and ordinary people bear much of its cost. The player travels through contrasting regions, including White Orchard, Velen, Novigrad and the Skellige Isles.

The world is not simply a backdrop: local history, prejudice, poverty and power struggles shape the stories Geralt encounters.

03

How a Witcher fights

Combat blends swordplay, dodging, parrying, preparation and supernatural abilities called Signs. Geralt uses two swords: steel is generally used against human and non-human foes, while silver is associated with many monsters.

Players can prepare with potions, oils and bombs. Learning a creature's habits and weaknesses can make a hunt more manageable.

04

Choices have consequences

Quests often ask the player to interpret incomplete information and choose between competing interests. Some outcomes are immediate; others emerge later. A decision that seems straightforward may carry an unexpected cost.

This moral uncertainty is the inspiration for our interactive Leshen investigation.

Game dossier / Know the original

Inside The Witcher 3.

A spoiler-light guide to the game behind this experience: its story, people, world, systems and expansions.

The game at a glance

A story-driven open world RPG

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is a single-player open-world role-playing game developed by CD PROJEKT RED and released in 2015. It is based on the fantasy universe created by Polish author Andrzej Sapkowski.

You play as Geralt of Rivia, a professional monster hunter known as a Witcher. Geralt is searching for his adopted daughter Ciri, whose extraordinary powers have drawn the attention of the spectral Wild Hunt. The search takes him through war-torn lands, crowded cities, villages, islands and dangerous wilderness.

The game is designed around exploration, character relationships, quests and consequential decisions. Many stories resist simple answers: a choice that appears kind in the moment may have painful consequences later.

A WORLD OF STORIES AND CONSEQUENCES
The protagonist

Geralt of Rivia

A mutated and trained monster hunter. He relies on swordsmanship, preparation, alchemy and Witcher Signs—but his judgement is tested as often as his fighting skills.

The central search

Ciri

Geralt's adopted daughter, a skilled young woman with a mysterious Elder Blood heritage. Finding her is the emotional thread running through the main story.

The threat

The Wild Hunt

A terrifying spectral force pursuing Ciri. Its presence gives the main story urgency and connects personal relationships to a much larger danger.

Important allies

Yennefer & Triss

Powerful sorceresses with deep histories with Geralt. Their relationships, loyalties and choices add personal complexity to the wider conflict.

How the game plays

More than sword fighting

Monster contracts: Gather information, inspect clues, identify a creature and prepare before the hunt. The investigation often matters as much as the battle.

Combat: Use steel swords against human opponents and silver swords against many monsters, alongside dodges, parries and tactical preparation.

Witcher Signs: Cast simple magical abilities such as Aard (a force blast), Igni (fire), Yrden (a magical trap), Quen (a protective shield) and Axii (a mind-influencing sign).

Alchemy and equipment: Prepare potions, oils and bombs; improve armour and weapons; and adapt your equipment to the threat.

What makes it distinctive

Choices with consequences

Quests frequently present competing interests rather than an obvious heroic answer. The game explores prejudice, war, grief, family, power and the cost of survival.

Its open world encourages players to leave the main path, discover side stories and meet people whose lives are shaped by the surrounding conflict. Even a seemingly small contract can reveal a complicated human story.

For this project: our forest-first investigation reflects the game's emphasis on gathering clues and understanding context before deciding how to act. Our branching Leshen scenario is an original classroom interpretation, not an official quest.

The complete adventure

Main game & expansions

The main adventure is expanded by two major story expansions.

Base game · 2015

The Wild Hunt

Geralt searches for Ciri while navigating the Northern Kingdoms and the threat of the Wild Hunt. The main game combines a central narrative with a huge range of side quests and contracts.

Expansion · 2015

Hearts of Stone

A darker, character-focused story involving a mysterious contract, a dangerous bargain and the enigmatic Gaunter O'Dimm.

Expansion · 2016

Blood and Wine

A substantial new adventure set in the colourful duchy of Toussaint, where a series of brutal attacks hides a deeper mystery.

Universe & source

Based on the books

The game continues the world of Andrzej Sapkowski's Witcher stories. It is a distinct video-game narrative, so the books and game should not be treated as identical versions of every event.

06 / Beyond the fantasy

Not all monsters are evil.

“Before you hunt a monster, make sure you understand what makes it a monster.”

The Witcher 3 invites players to look beyond appearances. Its world complicates the boundary between human and monster, and asks players to consider the consequences of their choices.

Our website reimagines that idea as an interactive field journal. The bestiary encourages observation, the map invites exploration, and the investigation asks visitors to gather evidence before judging.

Our central question: Are monsters always evil, and are humans always good?
