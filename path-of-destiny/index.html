02 / A world of difficult choices

Not every story has a hero.

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is an open-world role-playing game developed by CD PROJEKT RED. You follow Geralt of Rivia, a professional monster hunter travelling through a world shaped by war, fear, prejudice and ancient magic.

Here, monsters are not always the only danger. Political power, desperation and human choices can be just as destructive. The world asks a difficult question: who gets to decide what is monstrous?

05 / Beyond the fantasy

Observe first.
Understand before you judge.

The Witcher 3 invites players to look beyond appearances. Its world complicates the boundary between human and monster, and asks players to consider the consequences of their choices.

Our website turns that idea into an interactive field journal. The atlas invites exploration, the Bestiary encourages observation, and the contract asks visitors to gather evidence before making a decision.

Our central question: Are monsters always evil, and are humans always good?
