How this revision room works

1. Answer like an exam. Choose True or False. The feedback explains the exact detail that makes a statement correct or incorrect.

2. Wrong answers become revision targets. Each incorrect attempt is automatically placed in the Error Questions tab, together with the correct answer and explanation.

3. Return later. Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage. Leaving the page or closing the tab should not erase the record, as long as you return to the same browser and device and do not clear its site data.

Restart whenever you want. Use “Restart from beginning” to return to Question 1 and the full question bank. This does not erase your saved answers or study record. To erase everything and start with a blank record, use “Reset all progress” in the Study Record tab.

4. Re-test your weak points. Use Error Questions to retry only the statements you previously missed. Correct answers remove them from the active error list after a successful retry.

Teacher's standard. Questions test close reading, sequence, character identification, factual details, quotation attribution, symbolism, and interpretation. The interpretation questions follow the framing in your study notes rather than pretending every symbol has only one fixed meaning.

Generated lecturer practice. After the current bank, press “Generate 10 more questions”. The site combines two different source-based claims into a single True/False question. Each pairing is unique, has a worked explanation, and is saved so it remains in your record when you return. There are thousands of possible pairings; once every unique pair has been used, the site will tell you.

Privacy note: the record is stored on this device in this browser; it is not synchronised to another device or account. Use Download my record for a portable backup.
