I.
Act one · The stage

Welcome to Fontaine.

Where theatre meets justice, elegance conceals conflict, and the eyes of a nation are always watching.

The nation of justice · Teyvat
A world of spectacle

All the world is watching.

Genshin Impact invites us into Teyvat, a fantasy world of distinct nations, histories, and elemental powers. Among them is Fontaine: a place shaped by French-inspired elegance, opera, legal institutions, innovation, and water.

Here, justice is not merely delivered. It is staged. Every trial becomes a spectacle, every story a public performance.

Tonight, you are not simply an audience member. You are part of the story.

II.
Act two · The role

Choose the face of Furina.

A celebrated figure of Fontaine. Confident, dramatic, charismatic. But is the person on stage the whole story?

Choose a card. The curtain reveals only what you are willing to look for.
III.
Act three · The trial

What would you choose?

Three original dilemmas inspired by the themes of Furina’s story. There is no perfect answer—only decisions, consequences, and the values behind them.

Your choices will be reflected here as you move through the trial.

IV.
Act four · Behind the mask

The curtain falls.

The spectacle fades. What remains when the audience can no longer see you?

A moment of truth

“What if the person everyone admired was never allowed to be herself?”

What if every smile was part of a performance?
What if the greatest sacrifice was one that nobody could truly understand?

For centuries, the performance continued. The world watched. The world applauded. But the person behind the mask remained alone.

Eventually, the curtain fell. And for the first time, she no longer had to perform.
