01 / The adventure

More than a world map.

Genshin Impact is an open-world action role-playing adventure developed by HoYoverse. You play as the Traveler, an outsider searching across the fantasy world of Teyvat for a long-lost twin. Along the way, the journey becomes much bigger: nations, gods, ancient history and the people who live between them.

Exploration is part of the story. Climb mountains, glide over valleys, swim through rivers, discover hidden places, meet characters and take on challenges. The world invites curiosity rather than forcing you down a single road.

Explore. Discover. Connect.

07 / Original interactive story

The Lanterns of Qingce

A fan-made side story inspired by Teyvat's atmosphere. A village's lanterns are going dark, and the villagers disagree about why. Your choices shape the outcome.

Field story · Prologue

A village asks for help.

At dusk, a villager from Qingce Village asks you to investigate a series of lanterns that have gone dark near the terraces. Some residents blame a wandering spirit. Others believe the problem began after a recent storm. Paimon looks at you: “So, where should we start?”

This quest is original and non-canonical. It is not an official Genshin Impact mission or dialogue.

A thought to take with you

Is a nation defined by its Archon—or by its people?

Teyvat's stories repeatedly place ideals such as freedom, contracts, eternity, wisdom and justice under pressure. A useful discussion question is whether a nation's ideal is best understood through its ruler, its institutions or the everyday choices of its people.

Project reflection

Explore before you judge.

Our interactive activity asks players to gather evidence, listen to different perspectives and consider consequences before acting. This is a creative interpretation of exploration and storytelling—not a claim about an official game lesson.
